Cookies

Last updated 23 October 2026.

Essential cookies only. Moonbeam Academy uses cookies only to keep people signed in. There are no analytics, advertising, social-media, fingerprinting or other tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts, on any page — for teachers or children. Because every cookie is strictly necessary for a service you asked for, there is no cookie banner to click through. If we ever wanted a non-essential cookie, we would have to change this page and ask first; our automated test suite fails if an unlisted cookie or a third-party script appears.

Every cookie we set

CookieWho gets itPurposeHow longType
better-auth.session_tokenTeachers, district staff and parents/guardiansKeeps a signed-in adult signed in. Holds a random session token; the session itself is stored on our server.Ends after 30 minutes without activity, 8 hours at most (one school day), or at sign-outEssential
better-auth.two_factorAdults with two-step sign-inRemembers, between the password step and the code step of one sign-in, which sign-in is in progress. Holds a random identifier.10 minutes, or until the code step is doneEssential
lumos_studentStudentsKeeps a child in their short practice session on a shared device. Holds a random token; only its hash is stored on our server.The class's session length plus 10 minutes, never more than 60 minutes; deleted by “I’m done”Essential

Both cookies are HttpOnly (page scripts cannot read them), sent only to Moonbeam Academy, and marked Secure (HTTPS only) in production. The student cookie is also SameSite=Strict.

When a staff member signs out, our sign-in library also sends already-expired copies of better-auth.session_data and better-auth.dont_remember and better-auth.trust_device to make sure they are gone. We never set these.

Other storage on the device

If you switch on the easier-to-read font, the device remembers that choice in its local storage (lumos-readable). It holds no personal information and never leaves the device. On a shared student device, “I'm done” clears it along with everything else. The app also keeps a cache of its own static files (code, icons, the offline page) so it starts quickly on school Wi-Fi; that cache never contains a child's or a class's data.
