3. Acceptable use for schools

  • Add only students who are in your class, and only after the district's consent process (a student needs a consent record to log in).
  • Enter only what the service asks for. Never enter a Personal Education Number, birth date, address, or notes about a child.
  • Keep staff passwords private; do not share accounts. Share class codes only with your class, and make a new code if one leaks.
  • Do not try to access another class's or district's data, test the security of the service without written permission, overload it, or copy or reverse-engineer it.
  • Use reports to support teaching. Proficiency levels are the teacher's own judgment, set from the evidence the service shows; the service never assigns one by itself.
