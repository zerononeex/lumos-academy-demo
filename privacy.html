For parents and guardians: the short version

  • Your child uses Moonbeam Academy only through their school, in short practice sessions the teacher starts.
  • We keep your child's first name and last initial, their class, and their math practice (which tasks, what they built or answered, how much help they used, how long each took). That's it.
  • We never collect a Personal Education Number (PEN), birth date, address, photos, voice, fingerprints or face data, location, or anything written about your child.
  • No ads. No trackers. No selling or sharing of data. We do not build profiles of children.
  • The school district decides whether your child uses Moonbeam and holds the consent. To see, correct or delete your child's information, ask the school or district; we act on their instruction within 30 days.
  • If your child's teacher invites you, you can sign in to see your own child's practice summary. You see only your own child, never classmates.
  • Data is planned to be stored in Canada. Moonbeam is pre-pilot: no real student data is held yet.

Who we are and our role

Moonbeam Academy provides teacher-directed math practice for BC Grades 2–4. When a school district uses Moonbeam, the district is the public body responsible for its students' information under BC's Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (FIPPA), and Moonbeam acts as the district's service provider: we handle student information only to provide the service, only as the district instructs, and under a contract with the district. Moonbeam's own commercial activities are subject to Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). This policy is organised around PIPEDA's ten fair information principles.

1. Accountability

the Moonbeam Academy Privacy Officer, [OWNER TODO: privacy officer email and mailing address] is accountable for our compliance with this policy. Each district's own privacy office remains responsible for its decision to use Moonbeam and for its Privacy Impact Assessment; we supply a support pack for that assessment.

2. Identifying purposes

We use personal information only to: let a child log in to a teacher-started session on a shared device; present math practice and teach from the child's mistakes; show the child's teacher, and an invited parent or guardian, their progress in BC proficiency language; keep the service secure (including a record of which staff member viewed which child's data); and meet our legal obligations. We do not use it for advertising, marketing, product analytics, or training any AI model.

3. Consent

Children under 13 cannot meaningfully consent on their own. For school use, the district decides the authority for collection and, where it requires consent, collects it from parents or guardians through its own process. The teacher or district records in Moonbeam that consent was collected (who collected it and when). A child without a consent record cannot log in. If consent is withdrawn, the record is removed and the child can no longer log in; the district can also ask us to delete the child's data (see 9). There is no parent sign-up: a parent or guardian can have an account only if the child's teacher or the district invites them, and Moonbeam emails a parent only to send that invite or a password reset they asked for.

Never collected from a child

No Personal Education Number (PEN), no date of birth, no home address, no photos or video, no voice recordings, no biometrics (face, fingerprint, voiceprint), no free-text notes about a child, no messages between users, no location, no device or advertising identifiers, no behavioural analytics. The database has no place to store most of these. We do not build behavioural profiles, rank children against each other, or track children across sites or apps.

Name, email, a password hash (until district single sign-on replaces passwords for staff), role, which children a parent or guardian is linked to, two-step sign-in settings (the authenticator key is encrypted and recovery codes are stored only as hashes), and for each sign-in session its IP address and browser type (for security). Sign-ins, views and exports of a child's data are recorded in an audit log that cannot be edited.

5. Limiting use, disclosure and retention

We never sell, rent or share personal information, and never use it for advertising. A child's practice data is visible to the child, their own teacher(s) and their own invited parent(s) or guardian(s). District administrators manage the roster and consent records but cannot see children's learning data, except through a logged, time-limited emergency access for one child with a stated reason. Nobody sees learning data for a child without a consent record. We disclose information only to the subprocessors below (to run the service) or where the law requires it.

No analytics, ad networks, social pixels, session replay, font services or other third-party scripts are loaded on any page. Read-aloud uses only voices built into the device, so a child's name is never sent to a speech service.

How long we keep it (approved schedule, applied with each district)

  • Practice records: the current school year plus one school year, purged within 60 days after 30 June; proficiency summaries are kept at least one year after use (FIPPA s.31).
  • A student the district removes, or whose deletion is requested: deleted from the live database immediately (with a deletion receipt), and from backups within 35 days.
  • Staff accounts: until the district removes them, plus 30 days. Sign-in session records: 30 days after expiry.
  • Access audit log: two school years. Failed sign-ins on an email we do not recognise: 12 months. Security access logs: 30 days.
  • When a district's contract ends: a 30-day export window, then deletion of all of that district's data within 30 days and a written certificate of deletion.

The purge jobs for the time-based items are built and tested; they run as a deliberate, dry-run-first operator command, and are scheduled when Moonbeam is first deployed. No real student data is held today.

6. Accuracy

Teachers and districts maintain the roster. Correctness is calculated by our server, never accepted from the device. Proficiency levels are set by the teacher from that evidence; the software never assigns one.

7. Safeguards

Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and, in the planned hosting, at rest; each district's data isolated in the database itself (row-level security), with application checks on top; no staff self-sign-up; short student sessions with a clear “I'm done” that wipes the shared device; rate-limited login; an audit log of staff access to children's data. See Security & privacy for schools.

8. Openness

This policy, our cookie list, and our security summary are public. Districts receive our full Privacy Impact Assessment support pack, threat model and test results.

9. Individual access and correction

Parents, guardians and students can ask to see, correct or delete a child's information. Because the district controls school data, please ask the school or district; if you contact us, we will pass your request to the district and act on its instruction. We respond to access and correction requests within 30 days.

10. Challenging compliance and complaints

Contact the Moonbeam Academy Privacy Officer, [OWNER TODO: privacy officer email and mailing address]. If you are not satisfied: for school use, contact the district's privacy office and, if needed, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (OIPC BC); for Moonbeam's own commercial activity, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC).

If there is a breach

If a breach of security safeguards involves a district's students, we notify the district within 24 hours of confirming it, with a full report within 72 hours, so the district can meet its own duties. Where a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we report it to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and notify affected individuals as PIPEDA requires (for school data, in coordination with the district). We keep a record of every breach for at least 24 months.
